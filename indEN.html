The Gravity of Humanity

2026
oil on canvas
90x100

Gravity is that which draws people together, compelling them to influence one another.
Billions of people live side by side, their lives, thoughts, and actions touching one another.
We are drawn together and pushed apart; we seek freedom while remaining dependent on others. In this painting, human bodies form the Earth—a fragile sphere held together not by physical laws, but by the invisible bonds between people.
It is a story about a world in which there are no isolated victories or defeats—only the shared destiny of humanity.

Between Laughter and Silence

2026
oil on canvas
90x70

The figure of a clown reveals the contradiction between one’s outward performance and inner state.
Bright clothing and a tie contrast with a dejected posture. It is an image of solitude behind a mask of merriment—a moment when the laughter fades, leaving one face-to-face with oneself.
The painting explores the boundary between the role a person presents to the world and the feelings they conceal behind it.

Holding On

2026
oil on canvas
80x60

A painting about the inner struggle of a person who, despite pain and vulnerability, strives to hold herself together. By embracing her own body, the figure seems to seek support within herself.
It is an image of quiet strength, solitude, and the capacity to hold on—even when it feels as though there is no strength left.

Shelter

2023
oil on canvas
80x60

At the center of the painting stands the fragile figure of a child, facing away from the viewer and looking toward the light of hope.
The space surrounding the child resembles a tunnel, yet its walls are formed by the figures of soldiers. These defenders have become a living shield, protecting the child from the chaos and destructive force of war.
The oil brushstrokes highlight the contrast between the steadfast, stone-like resilience of the warriors and the fragility of childhood.
This work serves as a parable for a new understanding of "shelter"—where safety and the right to a future for an entire generation are provided by those who hold the front line.

The One Who Did Not Submit

2025
oil on canvas
80x100

The title of the painting highlights the strength of spirit of the central figure, who maintains his own stance amidst the crowd. It speaks to personal freedom, inner resilience, and a refusal to be part of a mass blindly heading somewhere.
The colors of the painting—a blue sky and yellow earth—evoke the Ukrainian flag, symbolizing peaceful skies and fertile land. This combination embodies the indomitability of the Ukrainian spirit: even amidst a torrent of hardships, there are those capable of thinking freely and choosing their own path.

Three "Nots" ...

2023
oil on canvas
100x70

This painting features a surreal theme: at its center is a head—wearing headphones and with a lock on its lips—encased within a cardboard box.
It represents a person isolated from the world, deprived of the ability to speak or hear anything beyond the bounds of what is permitted. Similar heads are scattered across a field of flowers, creating a paradox: life goes on, yet consciousness remains locked away. This is a reflection on modern society, where individuality is often devalued and thought is standardized.


About Me and My Work

My primary tools are color, compositional rhythms, and the texture of the brushstroke. Oil paints provide the plasticity and depth I need to capture the dynamics of human experiences—things often difficult to express in words.
The figures in my work are rarely portraits of specific individuals; rather, they serve as embodiments of human situations. By blending the serious with the ironic, I aim to leave space for the viewer to form their own interpretation and engage in a dialogue with the piece.
